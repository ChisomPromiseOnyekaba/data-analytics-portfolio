# 🚛 Logistics Performance Analytics Dashboard

### Power BI Capstone Project | Tech Sphere Academy | 2024

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This capstone project delivers a comprehensive **end-to-end logistics analytics solution** built in Power BI, covering 3 years of operational data (2022–2024) for a trucking and freight company.

The goal was to transform raw logistics data into actionable executive insights across five key business areas: revenue performance, fleet utilization, driver efficiency, fuel cost management, and safety compliance.

## 🔑 Key Business Insights

1. **Strong profitability** — A 66% profit margin indicates efficient cost management relative to gross revenue.
2. **On-time delivery gap** — At 45%, delivery performance is well below the 80% industry benchmark, suggesting logistics planning and route optimization opportunities.
3. **Fleet downtime concern** — Volvo trucks account for the highest downtime hours (12.3K), warranting a review of maintenance contracts or replacement planning.
4. **Fuel cost concentration** — Texas and Tennessee drive the highest fuel costs; route and load optimization in these states could improve margins.
5. **Safety risk** — 37.65% of incidents are preventable, and DOT violations lead all incident types. A proactive driver training program is recommended.
